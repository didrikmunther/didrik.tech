Pure pursuit, August 2020: a simulated car steering itself around a track. This is the original app.py, running in Python in your browser. The car keeps choosing a point where a circle around it meets the track, then steers along the arc that reaches it. Change the circle's radius to see how the line changes: small values hug the track, large ones cut corners, and too small loses it. Loading Python (about 9 MB)…
